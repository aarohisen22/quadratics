How to play

Controller

Left stick move · right stick aim · RT fire · RB throw shuriken · Y switch weapon · A jump · LB run · Start pause · Back/View map

Keyboard + mouse

WASD move · mouse aim · left click fire · right click or E shuriken · Q switch weapon · Space jump · Shift run · Esc or P pause · M map

Two players on one keyboard

Anay: WASD + mouse. Arha: arrow keys move, / fire, . shuriken, Right Shift jump, comma switch weapon.

Tips

Walk into supply crates for ammo, health and special weapons. Hide in the ruins or up a tree. In 2 players, stand next to your downed brother to revive him. Shoot trees to topple them onto samurai.

Arrows or stick to choose · Enter or A to pick · Esc or B to go back

Paused

Esc / P / Start: resume · arrows or stick + Enter / A to pick

Defeated

A or Enter: try again · B or Esc: menu
